📘 Sector Guide: Private, Civil, Teachers, Doctors & Uniformed

1. Private & Civil Service (7% Employee / 11% Employer)

Employees contribute 7% of basic salary toward pension, while employers/government contribute 11% (not deducted from employee net pay). OT multipliers are 1.5x, 1.75x, 2.0x, and 2.5x based on 208, 176, or 240 monthly hours.

2. Teachers & Doctors (Health Professionals)

Public teachers and doctors generally follow civil service pension rules (7% employee / 11% government). Doctors often receive Duty, Night/Weekend shift, or Risk allowances, and teachers may have teaching period schedules or school allowances—verify allowance tax exemptions with HR/finance.

3. Military & Police Services (7% Member / 33% Government)

Under Ethiopia's public service pension framework for military and police service, members contribute 7% while the government/institution contributes 33%. Operational duty pay and allowances follow specialized service regulations rather than standard commercial OT hours.

4. Employer Payroll Batch Mode

Organizations paying multiple staff can switch to the Payroll tab, enter each employee's sector, basic salary, and extra pay, and view combined totals for net pay, tax, 7% employee pension, and 11%/33% employer pension.
